## An attempt to master german

With LLMs being language geniuses and apps now cheap to develop, why not tailor an app for learning german?

- Enter words that come up during your every day life.
- Ask the LLM for tailored quizzes for grammar (when to use `vor` vs `seit`) or for vocab such as house-hold items
- Generate custom stories at your specified, e.g. A2 level
  - Listen to the story for **listening comprehension**
  - and also generate questions from the story to test your **reading/hearing-comprehension**.
